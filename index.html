Not another profiler

DatasetDoctor focuses on whether the dataset and evaluation design deserve to be trusted, then turns findings into reviewable actions.

Experiment Integrity

Compare random evaluation with chronological, forward, group-aware or cautious row-order sensitivity checks.

Leakage & Association

Separate direct leakage from identifier, temporal and provenance risks instead of treating every strong predictor as leakage.

Repair Lab

Preview repairs, compare before/after behavior and export without modifying the original dataset.

Stress Test

Measure degradation under missingness, numeric noise, feature loss and prior shift.

Synthetic Trust

Evaluate fidelity, downstream utility, diversity, class preservation and privacy-screening signals.

Dataset Contract / CI

Turn approved schema and quality assumptions into machine-readable checks for future dataset versions.
